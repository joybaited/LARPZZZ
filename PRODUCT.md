# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Visitors, gamers, and community members exploring LARPZ.

## Product Purpose
LARPZ website landing page showcase featuring an interactive 3D phone canvas, modular viewport sections, and high-impact display typography ("FAKE IT TILL' YOU MAKE IT").

## Positioning
An immersive, edgy brand landing page experience with full-bleed sections, custom 3D canvas, and distinctive violet/grape and deep tonal styling.

## Operating Context
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Three.js / React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber & @react-three/drei), GSAP, Lenis smooth scrolling.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Modular full-viewport sections (`min-block-size: 100svh` each)
- Three.js 3D phone model in hero
- Fluid typography and spacing scales solved between 390px and 1440px
- High-contrast typography with Megion and Neue Montreal
- Workbench routes for each section (/hero, /features, /pricing, etc.)

## Brand Commitments
- Colors: ink (#000000), paper (#ffffff), haze (#f5f2ff), deep (#201e45), plum (#433a67), mauve (#6d6189), lilac (#9d90aa), mist (#cbc9dd), violet (#eac2ff), grape (#3d065f)
- Fonts: Megion (display/bold), Neue Montreal (sans/body/medium)
- Headline: "FAKE IT TILL' YOU MAKE IT"

## Product Principles
- Immersive, tactile, crafted visual experience
- Every section is modular and view-height aligned
- Precision in fluid layout, type, and interactive 3D elements
